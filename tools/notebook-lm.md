# Notebook LM (Google)

## What It Is

NotebookLM is Google's AI-powered research and note-taking tool built on Gemini. It transforms your documents, videos, and web content into interactive, summarized knowledge bases with features like audio overviews, infographics, and deep research.

## What It's Used For

- Academic research and literature review
- Document analysis and summarization
- Study guide generation and learning
- Creating audio overviews (podcast-style discussions) from documents
- Content research and synthesis across multiple sources
- Converting documentation into accessible formats

## Key Features

- **Audio Overviews** — Converts documents into podcast-style discussions between two AI hosts
- **Deep Research** — Automates complex online research and generates comprehensive reports
- **Infographics & Slide Decks** — Creates visual presentations from source material
- **Multi-Source Analysis** — Examines relationships and connections across multiple sources
- **Diverse Source Support** — Google Docs, Slides, PDFs, text/markdown, web URLs, YouTube videos, audio files
- **Large Document Support** — Up to 500,000 words or 200MB per source
- **Collaborative Notebooks** — Share and co-edit notebooks (Plus plan)

## How to Get Started

1. Visit [notebooklm.google](https://notebooklm.google/) — no installation required
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Create a notebook and add sources (upload files, paste text, enter URLs)
4. Ask questions and get AI-generated insights
