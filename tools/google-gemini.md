# Google Gemini

## What It Is

Google Gemini is Google's multimodal AI model family that combines reasoning, understanding, and generation across text, images, video, and audio. It powers conversational AI, developer APIs, and deep integration across Google's product ecosystem.

## What It's Used For

- Research and multimodal queries across text, images, video, and audio
- Code generation, analysis, and debugging
- Document analysis and summarization (up to 1,500 pages)
- Content creation and creative projects
- Deep research with integrated web search
- Enterprise productivity via Google Workspace integration

## Key Features

- **Multimodal Processing** — Handles text, images, video, and audio inputs natively
- **1M Token Context Window** — Process extremely long documents and codebases
- **Image & Video Generation** — Create images and short videos from text
- **Deep Research** — Comprehensive research with integrated search capabilities
- **Google Workspace Integration** — Native support in Docs, Slides, Gmail, and more
- **Personal Intelligence** — Connect to Gmail, Photos, YouTube for personalized assistance
- **Multiple Model Tiers** — Pro, Flash, and Lite variants for different needs

## How to Get Started

**Web:** Visit [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) with a Google account

**Mobile:** Integrated into the Google app on iOS and Android

**API:** Access via [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com) or [Vertex AI](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai) for enterprise

**SDKs:** Available for Python, Node.js, Go, Dart, Java, and more
